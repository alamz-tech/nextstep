Good question. Spit wey no get blood, sweat, tears and urine no dey carry real HIV risk. So normal things like kiss, hug or to share cup no be how HIV dey spread.

HIV dey pass through:
• sex without condom (na so most HIV for Nigeria dey pass),
• blood, like needle wey una share, or sharp tool wey dem use for tattoo or tribal mark,
• from mama go her pikin when she get belle, when she born, or when she dey give breast.

People wey get HIV wey their treatment don make the virus very low no dey pass HIV through sex.
