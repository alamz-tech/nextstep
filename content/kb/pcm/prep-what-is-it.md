PrEP na HIV medicine wey person wey no get HIV dey use *before* e meet HIV, to reduce the chance say e go catch am.

• Nigeria guideline talk say if you use am as dem tell you, PrEP fit reduce HIV risk pass 90%. WHO talk say the protection go drop well-well if you no use am as dem tell you, so how well e go work depend on how you use am as health worker explain.
• Young people wey dey do sex na one of the groups wey Nigeria list for PrEP.
• WHO still list one ring wey woman dey put for inside as another PrEP choice.
• Nigeria guideline talk say PrEP dey work better if you join am with other protection, like condom.
• Health worker go first test you for HIV and check if PrEP good for you.

If you think say you meet HIV for inside the last 3 days, ask about PEP: na different medicine.

You wan find place to talk about PrEP?
