• HIV clinic, antenatal clinic, reproductive health clinic and STI clinic.
• Centre wey dey friendly for teenagers and young people.
• Some community centres. WHO still support make dem dey give PEP for places like pharmacy.

Test must be private and only if you agree. If person hurt you, ask for counselling and support too.
