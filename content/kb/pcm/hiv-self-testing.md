HIV self-test dey make you collect your own sample (mouth water or small blood), do the test and read the result for private, alone or with person wey you trust.

• Self-test na screening test, e no be final answer.
• If result show reactive (line for HIV), you need more test for clinic or with trained tester.
• If e no reactive, you fit trust am. But if you fit don meet HIV for inside the last 6 weeks, or risk still dey, test again later.
• Self-test suppose be your own choice. Nobody suppose force you.
