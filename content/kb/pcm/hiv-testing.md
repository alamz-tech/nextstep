HIV test na the only way to know your status.

• For Nigeria, HIV test must be wetin you agree to, and e must be private, with counselling.
• If you just meet HIV, e fit too early for test to show am. Dem dey call am window period.
• So after risk wey just happen, Nigeria guideline talk say make you test again: like when e reach 6 weeks, 3 months and 6 months after. People wey risk still dey fit test every 3 to 6 months; people wey risk low, like once every year.
• If result na positive, dem go connect you to care and treatment.

You wan find place to do test?
