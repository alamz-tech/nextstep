PEP na HIV medicine wey dem dey use *after* you fit don meet HIV, like sex without condom, condom wey burst, or sex wey person force you do.

Time na the most important thing. WHO talk say make you start PEP as quick as possible: e better within 24 hours, and no pass 72 hours (3 days) after wetin happen. Nigeria guideline still talk say as quick as possible, within 72 hours.

• The full PEP course na 28 days.
• No wait to know the other person HIV status. Go now.
• If dem force you, clinic fit also give you emergency contraception and support. No be your fault.

You wan find the clinic wey near you pass now?
