Condoms protect against HIV, other STIs and pregnancy at the same time.

• There are male condoms and female condoms. Both help.
• They work best when used correctly, every time you have sex.
• If you use lubricant, use one that is safe with condoms.
• If a condom breaks or slips off, PEP can help prevent HIV. Start it as soon as possible, and no later than 72 hours.
