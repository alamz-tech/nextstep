An HIV self-test lets you collect your own sample (mouth fluid or a drop of blood), do the test and read the result in private, alone or with someone you trust.

• A self-test is a screening test, not a final diagnosis.
• If the result is reactive (shows a line for HIV), you need more testing at a clinic or by a trained tester.
• A non-reactive result is reliable. But if you might have been exposed in the last 6 weeks, or you are still at risk, test again later.
• Self-testing should always be your choice. Nobody should force you.
