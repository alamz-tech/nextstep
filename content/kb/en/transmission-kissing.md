Good question. Saliva without blood, sweat, tears and urine do not carry a real risk of HIV. So everyday contact like kissing, hugging or sharing cups is not how HIV spreads.

HIV is passed on through:
• sex without a condom (most HIV in Nigeria is passed on this way),
• blood, for example shared needles, or sharp tools used for tattoos or tribal marks,
• from a mother to her baby during pregnancy, birth or breastfeeding.

People with HIV whose treatment keeps the virus very low do not pass HIV on through sex.
