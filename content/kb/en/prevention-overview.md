Here are ways to protect yourself. Using more than one together works best:
• Condoms, used correctly every time.
• PrEP: HIV medicine for people who may be at higher risk. A health worker checks if it's right for you.
• Testing: know your status, and your partner's.
• PEP: if you might have been exposed, start it as soon as possible, and no later than 72 hours.
