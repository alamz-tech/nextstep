PrEP is HIV medicine that a person without HIV uses *before* any contact with HIV, to lower the chance of getting it.

• Nigeria's guidelines say that, used as directed, PrEP can cut the risk of HIV by more than 90%. WHO notes that protection is much lower when it is not used as directed, so how well it works depends on using it as the health worker explains.
• Young people who are having sex are one of the groups Nigeria lists for PrEP.
• WHO also lists a vaginal ring as a PrEP choice for women.
• Nigeria's guidelines say PrEP works even better together with other protection, like condoms.
• A health worker tests you for HIV first and checks if PrEP is right for you.

If you think you were exposed to HIV in the last 3 days, ask about PEP instead: that is a different medicine.
