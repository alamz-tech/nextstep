Nigeria's guidelines list these places for PrEP, and testing is part of the PrEP service:
• HIV clinics, and antenatal, reproductive health and STI clinics.
• Adolescent and youth-friendly centres.
• Some community centres. WHO also supports giving PEP in places like pharmacies.

Testing must be confidential and only with your agreement. If someone hurt you, ask for counselling and support too.
