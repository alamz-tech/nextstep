An HIV test is the only way to know your status.

• In Nigeria, HIV testing must be voluntary and confidential, with your consent and with counselling.
• Very soon after a contact with HIV, a test can be too early to show it. This is called the window period.
• So after a recent risk, Nigeria's guidelines say to test again: for example at 6 weeks, 3 months and 6 months after the contact. People with ongoing risk may retest every 3 to 6 months; people with low risk, about once a year.
• If the result is positive, you will be connected to care and treatment.
