PEP is HIV medicine used *after* a possible contact with HIV, for example sex without a condom, a condom that broke, or sex you were forced into.

Timing matters most. WHO says PEP should be started as soon as possible: ideally within 24 hours, and no later than 72 hours (3 days) after the contact. Nigeria's guidelines also say as soon as possible, within 72 hours.

• The full PEP course lasts 28 days.
• Don't wait to find out the other person's HIV status. Go now.
• If you were forced, a clinic can also offer emergency contraception and support. It is not your fault.
